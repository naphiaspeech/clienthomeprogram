Say it

Listen to the model, then say it 3 times. Tap a circle each time you say it well. The marked letters are your target sounds.

先聽，再自己說3次。說得好就按一個圓圈。有底線的字母是要練的音。

Sound
Check yourself

Record yourself on your phone, then play it back. Do you hear the s at the end? Does sh sound different from s?

用手機錄音再聽一次，檢查自己的發音。

Build it

Tap the words in the right order. Then say the sentence out loud.

按正確次序點選字詞，排好句子後大聲讀出來。
